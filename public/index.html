About the activity

This independently developed, digital teaching adaptation is conceptually inspired by Ellis, B. J., & Kelley, H. H. (1999). The pairing game: A classroom demonstration of the matching phenomenon. Teaching of Psychology, 26, 118–121.

All profiles and ratings are fictional. Matching is based on reciprocal decisions, not an automated compatibility algorithm. Scores are simplified teaching prompts, not measures of people's worth.
